GOOD TO KNOW

Before you
hit upload.

Does BeatUploader upload automatically? +

No. You connect your account and start each upload yourself. Review the title and details before sending a video.

Which channel receives the video? +

The YouTube channel you authorize during Google sign-in. Check the connected channel shown in the app before uploading.

What does the app need? +

A Windows computer, an audio export, cover artwork, an internet connection and a Google account with a YouTube channel. Check the release notes for additional requirements.

Where can I learn how account data is handled? +

Read the BeatUploader privacy notice. OAuth access is used for YouTube features you initiate.
