Good to know

Before you
hit upload.

Does BeatUploader upload automatically? +

Only when you press Upload Beat. You connect your account once, then start each upload yourself after reviewing the title and details.

Are my uploads public? +

New uploads start as private. Open YouTube Studio to choose the visibility you want.

Which channel receives the video? +

The YouTube channel you authorize during Google sign-in. Check the connected channel shown in the app before uploading.

Can I set a custom thumbnail? +

Yes, add one in the Thumbnail tile. YouTube may require your channel to be verified before it accepts custom thumbnails.

What does the app need? +

A Windows computer, an audio export, cover artwork or a background video, an internet connection and a Google account with a YouTube channel. Check the release notes for additional requirements.

Where can I learn how account data is handled? +

Read the BeatUploader privacy notice. OAuth access is used for YouTube features you initiate.
