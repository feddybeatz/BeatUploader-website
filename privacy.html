Privacy notice

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This notice explains how BeatUploader (“the app”) handles information when you use its YouTube connection and upload features. BeatUploader is an independent product by Feddy Beatz. The app uses Google sign-in and the YouTube Data API with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly. It uploads the videos you choose and reads your channel name. The sign-in token is stored only on your PC at %APPDATA%\FeddyBeatz\BeatUploader. Nothing is sent to or stored on Feddy Beatz servers. You can revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. See the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). BeatUploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information the app uses

When you choose to connect a YouTube channel, the app uses Google OAuth to receive permission to access the YouTube features you authorize. It uses this authorization to identify the connected channel and to upload a video and its title, description, and tags when you initiate an upload. The current app version submits uploads as unlisted; you can review or change visibility in YouTube Studio.

The app processes the audio file, cover image, and metadata you select on your computer to render and upload your video. Those files are not sent to a BeatUploader-operated server. The video and metadata are sent directly from your computer to Google/YouTube for the upload you request.

Local token storage

To keep your channel connected between sessions, BeatUploader stores OAuth access and refresh tokens and the channel name locally in your Windows user profile, under %APPDATA%\FeddyBeatz\BeatUploader\youtube_tokens.json. These tokens are sensitive: protect access to your Windows account and device. You can disconnect from YouTube within the app, which removes this local token file. You can also revoke access through Google Account permissions.

Sharing and third parties

BeatUploader does not operate a service that collects your Google login password or stores your selected videos. Google and YouTube receive the information required to authenticate and complete an upload under their own privacy policies and terms. The app uses YouTube API Services; use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

OAuth tokens remain in the local app-data folder until you disconnect, remove the file, or uninstall/clean up the app data. Videos you upload are stored and managed by YouTube according to the upload visibility described above and YouTube account settings. BeatUploader does not have a server-side account or copy of your upload.

Children and changes

BeatUploader is intended for beatmakers and is not directed to children. This notice may be updated as app features change; the current version will be published on this page.
